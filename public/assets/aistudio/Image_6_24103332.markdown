## Brand & Style

This design system serves an academic knowledge base and educational encyclopedia focused on Systems Analysis and Design (Analisis dan Perancangan Sistem Informasi / APSI). It bridges the structured, reference-grade rigor of a scholarly encyclopedia (drawing from Wikipedia's informational clarity) with the distraction-free search utility and functional minimalism of modern productivity tools.

The target audience comprises computer science and information systems students, academic researchers, lecturers, and systems analysts. The interface evokes credibility, intellectual calm, focus, and structural order. 

The aesthetic is **Modern Editorial / Scholarly Minimalist**:
- High-contrast, legible typography pairing an authoritative editorial serif for long-form reading and knowledge headers with an ultra-clean, neutral sans-serif for UI controls, navigation chrome, and metadata.
- Clean paper-like surfaces, understated separation lines, and strict avoidance of decorative skeuomorphism or loud gradients.
- Visual elements emphasize density without clutter, hierarchical content navigation (tables of contents, breadcrumbs, cross-references), and precise metadata taxonomy (SDLC stages, UML modeling categories, design methodologies).

## Colors

The color palette prioritizes high-contrast legibility, cognitive endurance for long reading sessions, and clear categorical signaling.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (`#1E40AF` - Academic Deep Navy):** Represents authoritative presence and brand identity. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation markers, key section labels, and prominent academic iconography.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Scholarly Blue):** Used for standard text links, focused outline rings, and interactive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#0D9488` - Deep Teal):** Serves analytical callouts, system methodology classifications, and verified state indicators.
- **Neutrals:**
  - Base Ink / Heading: `#0F172A` (Slate 900) for uncompromised readability against light surfaces.
  - Body Text: `#334155` (Slate 700) for sustained reading comfort.
  - Muted Text & Meta: `#64748B` (Slate 500) for timestamps, author attributions, and citation counts.
  - Border Lines: `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) for table rules, panel dividers, and structural bounding.
  - Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) for document sheets and search containers; `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) for canvas backgrounds; `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) for sidebars, secondary panels, and code blocks.

### Domain-Specific Semantic Badges
- **SDLC Phases:** Deep Indigo (`bg-indigo-50 text-indigo-700 border-indigo-200`)
- **UML & Modeling:** Azure (`bg-sky-50 text-sky-700 border-sky-200`)
- **Methodologies (Agile, Waterfall, RAD):** Teal (`bg-teal-50 text-teal-700 border-teal-200`)
- **Publication Statuses:**
  - *Published / Peer-Reviewed:* Emerald (`bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border-emerald-200`)
  - *Draft / Working Paper:* Amber (`bg-amber-50 text-amber-700 border-amber-200`)
  - *Archived / Deprecated:* Slate (`bg-slate-100 text-slate-600 border-slate-200`)
- **Institutional Marker (IBIKKG):** Subdued Navy Slate badge with fine rule border (`bg-slate-900 text-white`).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a bifurcated architecture:
1. **Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`):** Applied to encyclopedic article titles, section headers (H1–H4), conceptual definitions, and reading lead paragraphs (`body-xl`). The font conveys scholarly weight, rhythm, and fatigue-free long-form reading.
2. **UI & Infrastructure Sans (`Inter`):** Applied to navigation, search query bars, tables of contents, technical metadata, labels, data tables, and interactive buttons.
3. **Monospace (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to data modeling representations, schema definitions, pseudo-code, and formal system specification grammar.

### Editorial Guidelines
- Use italicized serif styles sparingly, reserving them for formal academic definitions, translated terminology (e.g., Indonesian to English technical terms), or book/standard citations.
- Article body prose defaults to `16px` (`1rem`) on standard screens with an expansive `1.625` line height to ensure optimal scannability across multi-paragraph technical texts.
- Headings maintain strict structural hierarchy with automatic anchor linking.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an academic reading workbench:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 3-column asymmetric layout:
  - Left Column (240px–280px fixed): Navigation taxonomy, institutional attribution (IBIKKG), and topic trees.
  - Center Column (Max 780px fluid): Primary reading canvas formatted for optimal measure (65–75 characters per line).
  - Right Column (220px–260px fixed): Sticky on-page Table of Contents (ToC), active reading progress, and citation jump links.
- **Tablet (768px–1024px):** 2-column layout. The left navigation collapses into an off-canvas drawer; the ToC condenses into a sticky horizontal jump-bar.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with full-width search banner, sticky compact header, and drawer-based chapter navigation.

### Search-Centric Hero Layout
- Inspired by Google’s minimalist paradigm, global discovery lives within an unadorned, prominent search bar positioned centrally in header viewports or at the apex of root topic hubs, featuring instant type-ahead filters for UML diagrams, definitions, and SDLC phases.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows, opting for **crisp planar boundaries and low-contrast surface tiers**:

- **Depth via Tonal Surfaces:**
  - `Surface 0 (Canvas):` `#F8FAFC`
  - `Surface 1 (Document Base / Cards):` `#FFFFFF` with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
  - `Surface 2 (Sidebar / Inset Panels):` `#F1F5F9`
- **Ghost Outlines:** Structural containment relies on precise `1px` borders (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`) rather than dimensional shadows.
- **Elevation Overlays:**
  - *Dropdowns, Autocomplete & Modals:* Floating menus employ a discreet, non-distracting ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)` surrounded by a crisp `1px` border in `#E2E8F0`.
  - *Active Article Panels / Focused State:* Outlines transition to `#2563EB` with an ambient focus ring `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a restrained, semi-sharp geometry (`roundedness: 1` — Soft / 4px base):
- **Base UI elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges):** `4px` (`rounded` or `0.25rem`) for a precise, authoritative, and clean scientific edge.
- **Cards, Article Containers, Modals:** `8px` (`rounded-lg` or `0.5rem`) to soften larger surfaces without appearing overly playful or consumerist.
- **Search Input Bar:** Retains a subtle `6px` radius to maintain a modern, utilitarian aesthetic while matching the rectangular nature of reference tables.
- **Pills / Status Chips:** Strictly capped at `4px` to avoid hyper-casual capsule looks, preserving the aesthetic of formal catalogue indexing cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1E40AF`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `4px`, padding `0.5rem 1rem`. Hover: `#1D4ED8`. Active: `#1E3A8A`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Link:** Text `#2563EB`, background transparent, underline on hover.

### Global Search & Input Fields
- Input boxes feature a background of `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, and placeholder text `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: border `#2563EB` with `ring-2 ring-blue-100`.
- Includes instant shortcut badges (`⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`) positioned on the trailing end using `font-mono text-xs text-slate-400`.

### Badges & Taxonomic Tags
- Compact dimensions: font `11px` (`label-sm`), font-weight `600`, padding `2px 8px`, border-radius `4px`.
- Displayed with border stroke:
  - *SDLC:* `bg-indigo-50 text-indigo-700 border border-indigo-200`
  - *UML:* `bg-sky-50 text-sky-700 border border-sky-200`
  - *Methodology:* `bg-teal-50 text-teal-700 border border-teal-200`
  - *Status Published:* `bg-emerald-50 text-emerald-700 border border-emerald-200`
  - *Status Draft:* `bg-amber-50 text-amber-700 border border-amber-200`

### Institutional Badge (IBIKKG)
- Rendered in a fixed institutional lockup: Crisp rectangular unit with a solid `#0F172A` ground, `#FFFFFF` crisp uppercase tracking (`tracking-wider text-xs`), and an adjacent thin hairline separator with the institutional campus label.

### Encyclopedic Cards & Content Blocks
- **Article Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `1.25rem`. Transitions border color to `#94A3B8` on hover.
- **Definition Callout Box:** Left-accented card with a `3px solid #1E40AF` left border, background `#F8FAFC`, inner padding `1rem 1.25rem`.
- **UML Diagram Viewer Card:** Slate background (`#F8FAFC`), crisp bordered figure box with figure numbering (`Figure 2.1: Use Case Diagram`), caption in `body-sm text-slate-500`, and full-screen expansion toggle.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Table of Contents (ToC):** Clean vertical list, left-border indicator (`2px solid transparent`), transitioning to `#1E40AF` when scrolled into view. Text uses `label-md`, shifting from `#64748B` to `#0F172A font-semibold` when active.
- **Academic Citation & Reference List:** Indented hanging paragraphs with linked DOIs or retrieval links styled in `#2563EB`.
